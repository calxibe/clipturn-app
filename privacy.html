Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the ClipTurn Windows application handles on your device, what the clipturn.app website does, and what happens when you contact the publisher.

Plain-language summary: ClipTurn processes videos locally on your Windows device. The app does not upload your videos, require an account, serve ads, or collect analytics, telemetry, or crash reports. It keeps the local records needed to run jobs and prove results. The website uses Google Analytics to measure visits and interactions; that service uses first-party analytics cookies and receives the website information described below.

1. Scope and publisher

This Privacy Policy applies to ClipTurn version 1.0.5, the ClipTurn listing in the Microsoft Store, and this website. ClipTurn is published by FixQuotes (“FixQuotes,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

Microsoft’s privacy statements cover Microsoft Store, Windows, and other Microsoft services; they do not replace this policy for ClipTurn. Other websites and services linked from ClipTurn are governed by their own policies.

2. What the ClipTurn app processes

ClipTurn accesses a video only when you select it, drop it into the app, or invoke a ClipTurn command for it in File Explorer. Processing happens locally using Windows media components, locally installed codecs, and components included with the app.

The app may process:

  • the video and audio content of the selected file;
  • the filename and full path;
  • embedded metadata such as capture time, camera information, GPS location, track language, colour information, and HDR data;
  • container, codec, stream, geometry, duration, and device-capability information needed to plan and verify a rotation; and
  • file identity information such as size, timestamps, file ID, attributes, and a SHA-256 fingerprint.

A selected video may contain personal information—including identifiable people, voices, locations, or timestamps. ClipTurn handles that content only to perform, recover, report, and verify the operation you requested.

3. Information stored locally

ClipTurn stores operational records in the app’s Windows package data folder on your device. It does not write verification receipts into the video, an alternate data stream, or a sidecar file beside the video.

Local informationWhy it is usedTypical retention
Settings Theme, processing policy, concurrency, backup, timestamp, and failed-file preferences. Until changed or the app’s packaged data is removed.
Queue, activation, job, and recovery records Run work safely, recover interrupted operations, hand a durable job to the app for review, and show outcomes. Records can include full source paths, job IDs, and result messages. Queue and recovery items are cleaned up when safely completed. Durable job history has no automatic expiry and remains until packaged data is removed.
Verification receipts Bind a result to the exact file using path, identity, size, timestamps, attributes, SHA-256, app version, outcome, and proof flags. No automatic expiry; retained in packaged data until that data is removed.
Working files Create, verify, and atomically commit a candidate while protecting the source. Normally deleted after completion. Failed candidates may remain if you enable that option, and recovery artifacts can remain when cleanup cannot safely finish.
Optional backups Keep a copy when you enable in-app backup. Stored with your files until you delete them. They are outside packaged app data.

If you use an explicit “Copy path” or “Copy report” action, Windows clipboard content may remain after ClipTurn closes until you replace it or Windows clears it.

4. Uploads, sharing, and external services

No ClipTurn app upload or tracking service

ClipTurn does not send videos, filenames, paths, media metadata, settings, job records, verification receipts, analytics, telemetry, or crash reports to FixQuotes. The app has no account system, advertising system, or cloud-processing service.

Microsoft Store and Windows

Microsoft distributes and updates the app. When you use Microsoft Store or Windows, Microsoft may process account, device, license, download, reliability, or diagnostic data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. ClipTurn itself does not receive Microsoft error-report data in version 1.0.5.

Codec and help links

If you choose a remediation link, ClipTurn opens an allowlisted Microsoft Store product page or Microsoft support page. These fixed links do not include your file, path, or video data.

Synced folders

ClipTurn does not upload files. If you rotate a fully available file inside OneDrive or another synced folder, your sync provider may upload the changed file as part of that provider’s normal operation. Review the provider’s settings and privacy policy.

5. The clipturn.app website

This website is delivered as static files. It has no account or contact form, downloads no web fonts, and uses the typefaces already installed on your device. Its screenshots and the tutorial video's preview image are hosted with the site. Every page also loads the Google tag described below when the page opens.

Google Analytics

clipturn.app uses Google Analytics 4 with measurement ID G-26WWFWZZMP to understand how many people visit, which pages and referrals are useful, and how the website performs. This measurement applies only to website activity. The tag is not part of the ClipTurn app and does not receive your selected videos, filenames, local paths, app settings, job records, or verification receipts.

The standard Google Analytics implementation can collect page addresses and titles, referral information, session and interaction statistics, approximate location, and browser, device, language, and screen information. Depending on the Google Analytics web-stream settings, measured interactions can include page views, scrolling, outbound-link clicks, file downloads, and similar website events. Google Analytics stores a pseudonymous client identifier in first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_26WWFWZZMP to distinguish visitors and sessions. These identifiers are not a ClipTurn account and FixQuotes does not ask Google Analytics to collect your name or email address.

Your IP address is necessarily sent with the network request to Google's collection system. Google states that Google Analytics 4 uses it at collection time to derive approximate location and then discards it before the IP address is logged or stored in Analytics. Google Analytics is provided through Google Ireland Limited, and Google processes the resulting measurement data for FixQuotes under Google's Ads Data Processing Terms. Google also publishes its Privacy Policy and explains how it uses information from sites that use its services.

YouTube tutorial

The tutorial video on the home page is hosted on YouTube, but nothing is requested from the YouTube player until you press play. Google Analytics may already have received the separate website measurement described above, but the preview image and play button are ours and no YouTube playback request is made before your click. When you press play, the player is embedded from youtube-nocookie.com, and from that moment YouTube receives your request and applies Google's privacy policy rather than this one. You can watch it on YouTube directly instead, or simply not press play.

The home page loads external badges from Fazier, Product Hunt, Uneed, PeerPush, and directree when the badges approach the visible page. Those services may receive ordinary request information such as your IP address, browser identifier, the requested badge URL, and a timestamp. The requests include no ClipTurn file, video, or app data and are handled under Fazier's privacy policy, Product Hunt's privacy policy, Uneed's privacy policy, PeerPush's privacy policy, and directree's privacy policy.

As with any website, hosting and security infrastructure may temporarily process ordinary request information such as IP address, browser identifier, requested URL, timestamp, and security events to deliver and protect the site. Separate from the Google Analytics reporting described above, FixQuotes does not use those delivery and security logs for advertising or maintain an account-based ClipTurn visitor database.

Links to Microsoft and FixQuotes open separate services. Information you submit through the ClipTurn contact page is handled under the FixQuotes website privacy policy.

6. Retention and your controls

Windows removes ClipTurn’s packaged local settings, job history, queue data, and verification records when you uninstall the app. Uninstalling does not undo rotated videos or remove files outside packaged data, including optional backups, safely preserved recovery files, synced copies, or clipboard content.

You control app data by:

  • choosing which videos ClipTurn may open;
  • choosing whether the in-app workflow keeps a backup;
  • deleting videos, backups, retained failed candidates, or clipboard content that you control; and
  • uninstalling ClipTurn to remove its packaged local state.

Website analytics retention and controls

Google Analytics first-party cookies can remain in your browser for up to two years unless you delete them sooner or your browser limits them. User-level and event-level data in a standard Google Analytics property can be retained for up to 14 months; standard aggregated reports are not governed by the same user/event retention setting and can remain longer. Google may retain information needed to operate and protect its service under its own policies.

You can block or delete analytics cookies through your browser. Google also provides the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. These choices do not prevent you from reading the static website or downloading ClipTurn from Microsoft Store.

7. Security and children

ClipTurn uses local file-identity checks, staging, rollback handling, and independent verification to reduce the risk of replacing the wrong file or accepting an invalid result. No software or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure, so keep an independent backup of important files.

ClipTurn is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. The app does not create profiles or knowingly collect children’s information for FixQuotes.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information held by a controller, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and complaint rights. ClipTurn’s app records remain on your device and are not available to FixQuotes, so FixQuotes cannot retrieve or delete that local information remotely.

Website analytics uses pseudonymous identifiers rather than a ClipTurn account. FixQuotes may therefore be unable to connect an Analytics record to your name or email address without enough information to identify the relevant record. You may still contact us about Analytics processing or exercise any applicable rights using the link below.

For information you intentionally send to FixQuotes through a support channel, or for a question about website infrastructure, use the contact link below. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when ClipTurn, the website, hosting, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date. A future version that adds online video processing, app telemetry, crash reporting, accounts, or other data practices will describe those practices before they apply.
