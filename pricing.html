Questions answered

Pricing FAQ

Is ClipTurn really free today?

Yes. The complete current ClipTurn app is offered for $0. There is no ClipTurn trial timer, subscription, or paid-feature gate.

Is this a reduced free edition?

No. File Explorer integration, single-video preview, Batch Rotate, supported formats, processing policies, Recent Jobs, and verification are included in the current app.

Will ClipTurn always be free?

We are not making a lifetime-pricing promise. ClipTurn is free at the time this page was published. If that changes, Microsoft Store will show the applicable price and terms.

Are paid ClipTurn plans being announced?

No. This page describes one complete current app. It does not announce a Pro, Business, or subscription tier.

Do I need an account or payment details?

ClipTurn itself does not require an app account, request payment details, or provide an in-app checkout. Microsoft controls Store acquisition and any Store account requirements.

Can a required codec cost extra?

Yes. Some videos can depend on a separately offered Microsoft or device-manufacturer codec. ClipTurn does not sell, buy, or silently install those components; they are separate from ClipTurn’s current price.
